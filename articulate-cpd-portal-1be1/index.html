Live sessions

One fixed link for every live event

The live page never changes address, so it can go into a calendar invite weeks ahead. The stream embeds from Vimeo. Questions come in through the anonymous widget, so nobody has to put their name to a question in front of peers.

  • Permanent URL that works for every session
  • Vimeo live stream embedded in place
  • Anonymous Q&A, no names attached to questions
  • Session becomes a library replay when it ends

Completion tracking

The logbook fills itself in

Watch 80% of a video and the CPD activity is recorded. No quiz to pass, no form to submit, no certificate to chase. When a clinician needs their record, they export it as a PDF.

  • 80% watched marks the activity complete
  • Member-level record, not granular analytics
  • PDF export in the format the profession expects
  • Network-wide view of minutes watched for the administrator
