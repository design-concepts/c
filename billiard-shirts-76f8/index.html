Home / About

Our story

Built by someone who loves the game.

BilliardShirts.com is run by a pool player, for pool players. It started at the table, with a simple frustration: the gear on offer never looked as good as the game felt.

So we started making our own. Shirts you would actually wear to league night. Hoodies for the late sessions. Polos sharp enough for tournament day. Hats and mugs for everything in between.

Puzz Billiards is the first brand under our roof, born in Las Vegas in 2021. More brands from across the billiards world are on the way.

Made for players

Every design starts at the table and is made for the people who play.

Printed to order

Each piece is printed when you order it, so nothing goes to waste.

More brands coming

One home for the best billiard brands, starting with Puzz Billiards.
