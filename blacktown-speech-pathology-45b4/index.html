Funding options

Help with the cost of therapy

Speech pathology is a recognised Medicare health care service, and we work with NDIS and private health funding too.

NDIS

A registered and approved NDIS provider, offering early intervention and therapeutic supports for NDIA-managed, plan-managed and self-managed clients.

Medicare

All our speech pathologists are registered Medicare providers, eligible for EPC/CDM plan and Item 135 rebates with a GP referral.

Private health

Rebates may be available through your private health fund, depending on your fund and level of cover.

Not sure which applies to your family? Our client support coordinators can talk you through it.
