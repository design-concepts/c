Our story

What started as a side thing turned into the main thing.

Husband and wife duo Troy & Cerisa Benjamin didn't ever imagine becoming tea barons. But like most romances, tea popped out of nowhere and stole their heart.

Blak Brews is a little Australian tea co that's bringing people, health, culture & tastebuds together through a cup of the good stuff. Tea is the fun starting point. The bigger idea is putting First Nations and Blakfulla history in a positive light, one cuppa at a time.

“Blak Brews is working with a product that's been around for 60,000 years. It's not too new and crazy, just grounded, elevated, and special.”

Troy Benjamin, founder
2023
