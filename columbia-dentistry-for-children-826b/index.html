Your child’s first visit

A child’s first visit to the dentist should be enjoyable and positive. The more you and your child know beforehand, the better you will both feel.

When to come in

According to the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, children should visit the dentist by their first birthday. Newly erupted teeth (usually arriving at 6 to 12 months) benefit from proper care and good habits right from the beginning.

How we keep it calm

Children are not born with a fear of the dentist, but they can fear the unknown. We use pleasant, non-frightening, simple words to describe the visit and any treatment, so your family feels at ease from the moment you arrive.
