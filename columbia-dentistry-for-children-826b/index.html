Your child’s first visit

A first visit to the dentist should be enjoyable and positive. The more you and your child know beforehand, the better you will both feel.

  1. Come in by the first birthday

    The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends a first visit by age one. New teeth (usually arriving at 6 to 12 months) benefit from good care and habits right from the start.

  2. We keep it calm and simple

    Children can fear the unknown, so we use pleasant, non-frightening, simple words to describe the visit and any treatment.

  3. Finish at the prize tower

    Every patient gets to visit our three-sided toy vending machine on the way out.
