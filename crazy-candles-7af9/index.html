Our workshops

Dip, carve, spin and create

Every guest makes something to take home. Workshops are designed for all ages, with options tailored for pre- and primary school children.

Signature

Carved Candles

Dip, layer and carve molten, colourful wax into a one-of-a-kind candle, from classic shapes to race cars or crocodiles.

Retro favourite

Spin Art

Turn vintage vinyl records into colourful clocks with spin art, special paints and quirky embellishments.

Take-home gift

Soap Art

Personalise a premium soap base with moulds, colours and scents. A lovely gift, or a treat for yourself.
