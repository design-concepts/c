What we treat

Three areas of care, one clinic

We assess, diagnose and treat patients of any age, from general ENT conditions to specialised hearing and balance disorders.

Hearing and balance

Specialised care for hearing and balance disorders across the care continuum, including chronic ear disease and implantable hearing devices.

  • Cochlear implantation
  • Meniere's disease
  • Cholesteatoma
  • Stapedotomy surgery
  • Acoustic neuroma surgery
  • Implantable hearing devices
  • Superior canal dehiscence
  • Perforated ear drum
  • Exostosis
  • All hearing and balance conditions

General ENT

Snoring, tonsillitis, nasal obstruction and sinus problems, for adults and children.

  • Adult snoring
  • Nasal obstruction
  • Rhinosinusitis
  • Tonsillitis and peritonsillar abscess

Children's ENT

Children need special care. We work closely with parents so they are involved each step of the way.

  • Glue ear and grommets
  • Paediatric snoring
  • Hearing loss in children

Who treats you

Your surgeons

Prof Payal Mukherjee

MBBS, FRACS, MS, PhD

Adult and paediatric ENT surgeon with special skills in advanced ear surgery. Post-fellowship training in otology, cochlear implantation and lateral skull base surgery in Oxford and London.

Head of the ENT department at the Sydney Adventist Hospital, and Clinical Professor of Surgery at Macquarie University.

Care in one place

Hearing, balance and speech care alongside your ENT surgeon

The centre was set up to save patients the confusion, frustration and cost of moving between separate providers.

  • Neurosensory audiology

    Diagnostic tests and hearing solutions, including paediatric hearing tests, balance and dizziness assessments, and implantable devices.

  • Optimal Speech

    Speech pathology for patients with communication and swallowing disorders.

  • Vestibular physiotherapy

    Rehabilitation for inner ear deficits, with inpatient and outpatient care through the whole journey.
