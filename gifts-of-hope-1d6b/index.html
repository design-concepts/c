Hope for Christmas

Families across South King County apply each fall, and neighbors sponsor them directly. Families come from Skyway-West Hill, White Center, South Seattle, Everett and beyond to share in the season.

This year's family applications have closed and every family will hear from us. Sponsors can still choose a family now.

I Can HBCU: Dirty South College Tour

Every spring break, Gifts of Hope takes Seattle youth to visit Historically Black Colleges and Universities. For many, it's their first time leaving home, and they return with confidence, mentors and a clearer picture of their future.

I Can Rites of Passage

Over 18 weeks, youth build essential life skills before an educational journey to Africa. In 2026, scholars walked the Resilience Road through South Africa, from Table Mountain and Robben Island to the Apartheid Museum and Soweto.

Explore the program
  • Program length18 weeks
  • 2026 journey12 days, South Africa
  • NextJourney to Egypt
