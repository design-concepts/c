Meet the owner

Meet Dr Sonya Bains

A University of Sydney graduate with many years in small animal practice across Sydney's North West, and a Castle Hill local herself.

Sonya's interests are feline medicine and dermatology, with a strong passion for animal welfare. Our team builds long-term relationships with every family we care for.

I find it very rewarding to be able to improve the health and wellbeing of animals. I enjoy educating clients and involving them in the care of their pets.Dr Sonya Bains, Veterinarian BVSc
