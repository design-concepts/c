How it works

No waiting for appointments. No paying by the hour.

Members use the space independently, at their own pace, before or after workouts, workdays or anything in between.

Book your location and time

Choose a studio and a slot on the app, then self check in when you arrive.

Bring a suit and towel

Every location has changing rooms, lockers and showers. Rinse before each plunge.

Sweat and chill

Move between a 200° Finnish sauna and a 37–44°F cold plunge, maintained daily.
