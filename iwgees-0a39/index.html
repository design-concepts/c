Why endoscopic ear surgery

Place the surgeon’s eyes inside the ear

Angled endoscopes give a contextual understanding of anatomy that is difficult to match with the microscope. The aim is to use the best instrument, in the best way, for every patient.

  • 01

    An all-encompassing view

    The objective lens sits within the ear canal or middle ear, showing the disease and anatomy interface without removing normal bone and tissue.

  • 02

    One image for the whole theatre

    Everyone in the operating room sees the surgeon’s live image, lifting engagement and teaching.

  • 03

    See past the instruments

    The endoscope lets the surgeon visualise past the shaft of drills and curettes.

Membership

How to join IWGEES

A broad-minded, dynamic global community developing excellence in a new method of ear surgery. Applications are reviewed by the IWGEES board.

Perform EES regularly

Endoscopic ear surgery is part of your regular practice.

Attend a course

Attend, or have attended, at least one IWGEES-supported course, meeting or session.

Apply

Send a short CV and motivation letter, plus a recommendation from a member if you have one.

Board review

If approved, you are invited to join and listed on the IWGEES website.
