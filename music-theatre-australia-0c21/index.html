Meet the Director

You deal directly with the performer.

Music Theatre Australia is led by Rebecca Bode, an opera singer whose career spans the world's great stages. MTA isn't run by administrators. It's run by a working performer who knows exactly what it takes to move a room.

She doesn't just send you a list of acts. She advises, curates and tailors the entertainment to your event, your audience and the result you need.
