How it's made

Color, movement and a little bit of fire

Art has been my lifelong passion. Over the past decade I've pushed my work further, always experimenting with new techniques and materials so every piece stays fresh.

My goal is to evoke emotion and offer a unique experience with every piece.

To get that movement and texture, I work with:

  • Fluid acrylics
  • Stains
  • Water
  • Torches
  • Airflow
  • Gravity
