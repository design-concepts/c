Come for the cats. Leave with a friend for life.

At Rescued Treasures Cat Café, every cat you meet is waiting for a home. Relax with adoptable cats and kittens, enjoy free Wi-Fi and a snack, and help us save the community cats of Las Vegas.

Saving 500 cats in one year

With Best Friends Animal Society and The Animal Foundation, we aim to rescue 500 cats and kittens from the Las Vegas municipal shelter this year. Cats move into foster homes and the café, where they get medical care, socialization and adoption support.

  • Foster a cat in recovery
  • Adopt a cat from the program
  • Donate or volunteer to cover care and supplies
