Your hangar, your home, one taxiway from the runway.

Ridge Landing is a residential aviation community in Central Florida. Stabilized grass taxiways lead from every lot to a 3,000 ft paved runway with unlimited access for homeowners.

  • 99 lots, 1+ acre each

    Room for a custom home and private hangar.

  • Your builder, your design

    Work with the builder of your choice. We can recommend the best in the area.

  • Gated and secure

    A safe, gated community with a landmark entrance.

  • Surrounded by freshwater lakes

    Lagoons, preserved woodland and lakes. Ideal for amphibian pilots.

  • About an hour from the parks

    Disney World, Universal Studios and Busch Gardens are all within reach.

  • Florida tax benefits

    No state income tax, and no estate or inheritance tax.
