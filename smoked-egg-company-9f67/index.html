Why smoked eggs are different

A patented chilled smoking technique that stops the ageing process.

We have perfected the precise equation of time, temperature, humidity and volume of smoke.

World patent

The Smoked Egg Company holds the world patent on an advanced chilled smoking technique that stops the ageing process in eggs.

As fresh as the day they were laid

Laboratory tests indicate no bacteria at 18 weeks of age, compared to 21 million bacteria in a normal egg held in the same environment.
