About the author

From Mission Control to the written page

Steven began his NASA career at Johnson Space Center in Houston as a Space Shuttle flight controller in Mission Control, working alongside veterans of the Apollo Moon landings.

Today he serves as the Chief Engineer for Aeronautics in the Office of Chief Engineer at NASA HQ in Washington D.C. He lives in Mt. Airy, Maryland, and shares a passion for all things Star Trek.
