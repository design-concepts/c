The problem

Wiping it off is not the same as getting rid of it.

Mould grows where moisture sits. Treat the patch you can see and leave the cause, and it comes back.

That is why every job we do starts with moisture and humidity readings, not a spray bottle. Inspect. Correct. Protect.

What we treat

Mould remediation

Removal and treatment of household mould, with preventative recommendations for every room we treat.

Mould testing

A musty smell but no visible mould? Microbiological testing finds what you can't see. Charges may apply.

Ventilation, dehumidifiers and air purifiers

Humidity-sensing bathroom and laundry fans, plus equipment to keep moisture down once we've gone.
