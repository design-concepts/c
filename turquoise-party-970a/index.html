What is Turquoise Party?

New Mexico's premier circuit festival, bringing the community together for a weekend of music, connection, celebration and brotherhood.

Is Turquoise Party only for LGBTQ+ attendees?

No. Everyone is welcome. While Turquoise Party celebrates gay men, brotherhood and community, all respectful attendees and allies are welcome.

I'm coming alone. Will I fit in?

Absolutely. Many guests attend solo and leave with lifelong friendships and chosen family. If you'd like someone to meet up with at an event, reach out to Joshua, our director of production.

What is the atmosphere like?

An inclusive, high-energy celebration focused on music, community and connection, where everyone can feel safe, respected and free to be themselves.

Is there an age requirement?

All events are strictly 21 and over. A valid government-issued photo ID is required for entry.

Saturday, December 5, 2026 · Albuquerque

Santa Speedo Run

A little less fabric. A lot more holiday spirit. A festive 1.3-mile costumed fun run benefiting Casa Q, presented by Turquoise Party Foundation, Inc.

Register to Run Donate

Your afternoon, wrapped up

  1. 3:30 p.m.

    Check-in and hot cocoa

    At Buddies Barbershop. Meet fellow runners, get into the holiday spirit and gather for a group photo.

  2. 4:45 p.m.

    Let's run

    1.3 miles along Central's sidewalks from Buddies to VERS ABQ. A costumed fun run, not a timed race. Streets stay open, so stay on the sidewalks.

  3. ~5:00 p.m.

    Finish-line festivities

    At VERS ABQ. Photos, awards for standout costumes and Speedo spirit, then a festive Santa Kiki.

A fun run with a meaningful purpose

For Casa Q

Casa Q provides safe, affirming housing and support for LGBTQ+ teens and young adults in New Mexico. Register, then become a fundraiser through the Givebutter campaign and invite your community to support you.

Bring your holiday spirit

What to wear

Santa hats, Speedos and festive costumes are all part of the fun. Wear suitable shoes and bring warm layers. Tip: park near the finish at VERS and head to Buddies before check-in.

In loving memory

C.J. Chavez

November 14, 1976 to November 2, 2026

Finding inspiration in every dance.

C.J. Chavez was a co-founder of Turquoise Party and the founder of eVOLV Strong. He believed in strength, in the gym and in life, and he had a gift for helping others find their own. Whether he was coaching someone through a tough set or tearing up the dance floor, C.J. brought joy and energy to every room he walked into.

His spirit is part of every Turquoise Party weekend, and it always will be.

We are holding Adonis, C.J.'s family, and our whole community close as we grieve together.

Support his partner Adonis

Adonis's loved ones have set up a GoFundMe to support him during this time. If you're able, please consider giving or sharing.

Donate to the GoFundMe

Rest easy, C.J., we'll keep the music playing for you until we can dance together again.
Nick Griffith, President, Turquoise Party

Circuit with a cause

Our partner, Casa Q

A portion of all Turquoise Party proceeds is donated to Casa Q, which provides safe, affirming housing and support services for LGBTQ+ youth and young adults experiencing homelessness or housing instability in New Mexico.

Support Casa Q

Ages 14 to 17

Safe House

Immediate, long-term housing for LGBTQ+ youth experiencing homelessness or unsafe living situations, with crisis support, case management and referrals to medical, mental health and legal resources.

Ages 18 to 22

Transitional Living Program

Longer-term housing for up to two years, each client with their own studio apartment, plus case management, life skills, and education and employment support.

About Turquoise Party

Come for the music.
Stay for the brotherhood.

New Mexico's first major circuit festival, created as a home for gay and queer men seeking friendship, celebration and belonging, and open to everyone who supports the mission.

Mission

To create extraordinary experiences that foster brotherhood, meaningful connection and community while celebrating authenticity, joy and the spirit of New Mexico.

Vision

To become the premier circuit festival in the Southwest, known for world-class experiences that inspire connection, strengthen community and build lasting bonds of brotherhood.
