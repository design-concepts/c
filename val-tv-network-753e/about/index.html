About

Valerie McConnell

Founder and Independent Broadcast Distributor of VAL TV Network Sports & Entertainment, an independent television production and distribution platform based in Las Vegas, Nevada.

Valerie Glenzell McConnell has produced and hosted more than 100 television programs, covering professional boxing, sports, entertainment and community stories. VAL TV Network programming features interviews with athletes, coverage of major sporting events and original entertainment programming.

Distributed through

  • ▸Vegas Life TV
  • ▸The CW Network affiliates through Sinclair Broadcast Group
  • ▸YurView through Cox Communications

In addition to her broadcasting career, Valerie is part of the historic television legacy of Soul Train, created by Don Cornelius, where dancers and performers helped shape the global influence of music, dance and pop culture.

Today she continues expanding VAL TV Network Sports & Entertainment alongside Executive Producer Michael Childress Sr. and production partner ChilllSpotLV Productions.

On the beat

Signature coverage

Super Bowl Soulful Celebration Media Roundtable

An official media event connected to Super Bowl week. Valerie is entering her third year covering it, interviewing artists, athletes and community leaders. Coverage airs as special segments on VAL TV Network.

HBCU Las Vegas Classic mini-series

Documentary-style coverage of Jackson State vs. Grambling State that became a VAL TV Network mini-series on Cox Communications platforms: the game, the marching bands, the fans and the legacy of HBCU athletics.

Championship boxing coverage

Press conferences, training camps and championship fight events, with interviews with world champions such as Claressa Shields and fight-week coverage in Las Vegas and Detroit.

Las Vegas fight week

Press conferences, media days and promotional events from Premier Boxing Champions, Golden Boy Promotions and Top Rank.
