Meet your host

Valerie McConnell

Founder and Independent Broadcast Distributor of VAL TV Network Sports & Entertainment. Her break came in 2002, working with Don King. In March 2020 she launched her own production facility, VAL TV Studio, in Las Vegas.

She is also part of the historic television legacy of Soul Train, with official honors from the City of Chicago, the City of Los Angeles and the United States Congress.

  • Broadcast

    The CW affiliates via Sinclair, Cox YurView, Vegas Life TV

  • Honored

    US Congressional Recognition
